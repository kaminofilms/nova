Nova Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the Nova app ("Nova"), made by Kamino Films, collects and how it is used. Contact: kaminofilms@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You can use Nova to play games without signing in. Games, settings and saves stay on your device.

Nova account (optional)

If you sign in with Google to use Nova's social features, we store:

  • your Google account ID and email address, to identify your account;
  • the username, display name and avatar you choose, your friend code and your privacy settings;
  • your friends list, friend requests and people you have blocked;
  • messages you send to friends;
  • sign-in sessions (stored only as hashed tokens).

While you are online, your status and the game you are playing are shared with others according to your privacy settings, and are kept for about 90 seconds after you were last seen.

Voice chat

Voice chat is only possible between accepted friends. Audio is relayed live through our server and is never recorded or stored.

Cloud saves and Google user data

If you connect Google Drive, Nova requests only the drive.appdata permission. Your save backups are uploaded from your device directly to a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Nova can access only the files it created in that folder, not any other file in your Drive. The save files are never copied to Kamino Films' servers.

Our server keeps a small record of each backup so you can choose which one to restore: the game's ID, a revision ID, a checksum of the file, the Google Drive file ID and the backup date.

Nova's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide cloud saves to you. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Nova Nextendo (Nextendo Network sign-in)

The Nova Nextendo edition uses Nextendo Network accounts instead of a Nova account. You sign in on nextendo.network; Nova never sees or stores your Nextendo password. With your approval, Nextendo gives Nova only the permissions shown on its consent screen: your username, friend code, avatar and player ID, your friends list, friend requests and their online status, and permission to publish what you are playing. Nova keeps the Nextendo access and refresh tokens encrypted on your device only and uses them only to show your friends and status. Kamino Films' servers do not receive your Nextendo account data. You can revoke Nova's access at any time from your Nextendo account settings. Nextendo Network's own privacy policy applies to the data Nextendo holds.

Online play

When you play online through Nova's game servers, the game's online traffic passes through our servers so that multiplayer works. It is used only to run those services.

Security and abuse prevention

To limit abuse, our server keeps short-lived, hashed records of request addresses for rate limiting.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics SDKs in the app.
  • We do not sell personal information or share it with third parties, except the hosting providers that run our servers on our behalf.

Deleting your data

  • Nova account: email kaminofilms@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We delete your account, friends, messages and backup records within 30 days.
  • Cloud saves in Google Drive: open Google Drive on the web, go to Settings → Manage apps → Nova → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Remove Nova's access to Google: visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Nova's online features are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
