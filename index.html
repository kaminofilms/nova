Nova

An Android gaming app by Kamino Films

Nova runs games on Android phones and tablets, with performance modes tuned for each device. Playing never requires an account. An optional Nova account adds friends, messages, voice chat and cloud saves.

Features

  • Performance modes that pick resolution and graphics settings for your device
  • Friends, messages and voice chat with people you add
  • Cloud saves backed up to your own Google Drive

Cloud saves and Google Drive

When you connect Google Drive, Nova stores your game save backups in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive. Nova can only see the files it created in that folder; it cannot see or change any other file in your Drive. Your backups are never copied to Kamino Films' servers.
